Your last message did not land with the owner. Re-explain it:

- Plain, short Turkish sentences (law 3). Start with what it means for the owner: the result or the impact.
- Give the context the owner was missing: what happened before, why it matters, what is still open.
- Replace jargon with the terms in `docs/glossary.tr.md`; explain any remaining technical term in one clause.
- End with the decision the owner must make, if any, and who holds the next step.
